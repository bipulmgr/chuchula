Chuchula privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Chuchula is a card game by the developer named on its store page. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. Contact: SUPPORT_EMAIL

Offline play

Offline games against bots run entirely on your phone. Your settings, statistics, saved game and cash points are stored only on your phone and are deleted when you uninstall the app. Cash points are virtual. They cannot be bought, sold, withdrawn or exchanged for money or prizes.

Online play

Online play uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions), run by Google.

  • When you first open online play, Firebase creates an anonymous account: a random user id. We do not ask for your email, phone number or real name.
  • A room stores the name you entered in Settings, your avatar, your moves, the cards dealt and the score, plus timestamps so that players who drop out can be replaced by a bot.
  • Rooms are deleted automatically about two hours after their last activity.
  • Google may process your IP address and device information to run and secure these services. See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Bluetooth play (Android)

Bluetooth games connect phones directly. Nothing is sent to a server. Nearby phones can see your phone's Bluetooth name while you host or join a game. The app asks for the "Nearby devices" permission (on older Android versions, location) only to find and connect to those phones. It never uses this to work out where you are.

Ads

You can choose to watch a rewarded video ad to earn cash points. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising id, IP address, device information and how you interact with ads, to show ads and measure them. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent first and can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising id in your phone's settings. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not collect your contacts, photos, precise location or payment details.
  • The app has no in-app purchases and no real-money gambling.

Children

Chuchula is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on your phone. Online rooms delete themselves after about two hours. To ask about anything else, email SUPPORT_EMAIL.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address and in the app, with a new "Last updated" date.
