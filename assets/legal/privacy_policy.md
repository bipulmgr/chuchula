# Chuchula privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Chuchula is a card game by the developer named on its Google Play and App
Store pages. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. To contact
us, use the developer email address shown on the Chuchula store page.

## Offline play

Offline games against bots run entirely on your phone. Your player name,
avatar, settings, statistics, saved game and cash points are stored only on
your phone, together with a random id the app creates to tell your phone
apart in multiplayer games. Nothing of this is sent to us, and it is all
deleted when you uninstall the app. Cash points are virtual. They cannot be
bought, sold, withdrawn or exchanged for money or prizes.

## Bluetooth play (Android)

Bluetooth games connect phones directly. Nothing is sent to a server. The
players in the game see your player name, avatar and moves, and nearby phones
can see your phone's Bluetooth name while you host or join a game. The app
asks for the "Nearby devices" permission (on Android 11 and older, location)
only to find and connect to those phones. It never uses this to work out
where you are.

## Ads

The app shows ads from Google AdMob in two places: a video you can choose to
watch to earn cash points, and one full-screen ad when a whole match ends.
To show and measure these ads, Google may collect your device's advertising
id, IP address (from which an approximate location can be worked out),
device information and how you interact with ads.

- In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for
  consent first and can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad
  privacy choices.
- On iPhone, the app never asks to track you, so the advertising id (IDFA)
  is not used.
- On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising id in your phone's
  settings.

See how Google uses this data at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Online play

This section applies only to versions of the app that include Online play
(the home screen then shows a "Play online" button). Online play uses Google
Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions), run by
Google.

- When you first open online play, Firebase creates an anonymous account: a
  random user id. We do not ask for your email, phone number or real name.
- A room stores your player name, avatar, moves, the cards dealt and the
  score, plus timestamps so that players who drop out can be replaced by a
  bot.
- Rooms are deleted automatically about two hours after their last activity.
- Google may process your IP address and device information to run and
  secure these services. See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## What we do not do

- We do not sell your data or share it with anyone except as described
  above.
- We do not collect your contacts, photos, files, precise location or
  payment details.
- The app has no accounts to sign up for, no in-app purchases and no
  real-money gambling.

## Security

Data the app sends over the internet (ads, and online play where present)
is encrypted in transit by Google's services.

## Children

Chuchula is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect personal data from them.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on your phone. Online rooms
delete themselves after about two hours. For anything else, email the
developer address on the store page and we will reply within 30 days.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same
address and in the app, with a new "Last updated" date.
